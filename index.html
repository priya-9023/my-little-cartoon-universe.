Publish anywhere 🌍

What works now: create comics, export a picture, share using your phone's sharing menu (when supported), and back up your work as a JSON file.

What needs online setup: accounts, private cloud sync, a public gallery where other people can read stories, moderation, and a shareable public URL for each story.

To share to Instagram, Pinterest or other apps, export the comic and post it through that platform. This website does not automatically post to social media.
